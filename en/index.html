      How the groups are made

      Groups are as even as possible: sizes differ by one person at most. With rules on, the tool shuffles and swaps people to meet every rule. If a rule cannot be met, it tells you which one.

      The same draw number, list, and settings always give the same groups, so a shared link shows exactly what you saw.
